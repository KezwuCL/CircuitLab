Understanding Electrical Circuits

Learn the essential concepts behind electrical circuits and discover how voltage, current and resistance work together.

V

Voltage

Voltage is the electrical potential difference between two points in a circuit.

Symbol: V

SI Unit: Volt (V)

Formula: V = I × R

I

Current

Electric current is the rate at which electrical charge flows through a conductor.

Symbol: I

SI Unit: Ampere (A)

Formula: I = V / R

Ω

Resistance

Resistance is the opposition to the flow of electric current through a material.

Symbol: R

SI Unit: Ohm (Ω)

Formula: R = V / I

What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law describes the relationship between voltage, current and resistance in an ohmic conductor under constant physical conditions.

V = I × R

Example

A circuit has a resistance of 5 Ω and a current of 2 A.

V = I × R

V = 2 × 5

V = 10 V

Electrical Circuit Fundamentals

What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law describes the relationship between voltage, current and resistance in an ohmic conductor under constant physical conditions.

Voltage (V)

Voltage is the electrical potential difference between two points. It is measured in volts (V).

Current (I)

Current is the rate at which electric charge flows through a circuit. It is measured in amperes (A).

Resistance (R)

Resistance is the opposition to the flow of electric current. It is measured in ohms (Ω).

Electrical Formulas

Explore essential electrical equations, their variables and units.

V = IR

Ohm's Law

Voltage: V = I × R

Current: I = V / R

Resistance: R = V / I

V = Voltage (V)
I = Current (A)
R = Resistance (Ω)

Open calculator →
P = VI

Electrical Power

P = V × I

P = I² × R

P = V² / R

P = Power (W)
V = Voltage (V)
I = Current (A)
R = Resistance (Ω)

Power represents the rate of energy transfer. The resistance-based formulas apply to resistive components.

E = Pt

Electrical Energy

E = P × t

E = V × I × t

E = Energy (J)
P = Power (W)
t = Time (s)

These equations calculate electrical energy transferred at constant power.

Rₜ

Series Resistance

Rₜ = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ...

Rₜ = Total resistance (Ω)
R₁, R₂, R₃ = Individual resistances (Ω)

Total resistance is the sum of all resistances connected in series.

1/Rₜ

Parallel Resistance

1/Rₜ = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ...

For two resistors:

Rₜ = (R₁ × R₂) / (R₁ + R₂)

Rₜ = Equivalent resistance (Ω)
R₁, R₂ = Individual resistances (Ω)

Q = It

Electric Charge

Q = I × t

I = Q / t

t = Q / I

Q = Electric charge (C)
I = Current (A)
t = Time (s)

These equations relate electric charge, constant current and time.

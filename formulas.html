Electrical Formulas

Ohm's Law

Voltage: V = I × R

Current: I = V / R

Resistance: R = V / I

Electrical Power

P = V × I

P = I² × R

P = V² / R

Series Resistance

R(total) = R1 + R2 + R3 + ...

Parallel Resistance

1/R(total) = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3 + ...

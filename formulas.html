Electrical Engineering · Reference Sheet

Electrical Formulas

Explore essential equations used to solve electrical circuit problems.

V = IR

Ohm's Law

Voltage: V = I × R

Current: I = V / R

Resistance: R = V / I

Open calculator →
P = VI

Electrical Power

P = V × I

P = I² × R

P = V² / R

Power is measured in watts (W).

E = Pt

Electrical Energy

E = P × t

E = V × I × t

Energy is measured in joules (J).

Time is measured in seconds (s).

Rₜ

Series Resistance

Rₜ = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ...

The total resistance is the sum of all individual resistances.

1/Rₜ

Parallel Resistance

1/Rₜ = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ...

For two resistors in parallel:

Rₜ = (R₁ × R₂) / (R₁ + R₂)

Q = It

Electric Charge

Q = I × t

I = Q / t

t = Q / I

Charge is measured in coulombs (C).

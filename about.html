About CircuitLab

Connecting electrical engineering, programming and education.

About the Project

CircuitLab is an educational website designed to help students understand the fundamentals of electrical circuits and solve basic electrical equations.

This website was developed as a Capstone project to explore my interest in electrical engineering while improving my programming and problem-solving skills.

Guiding Question

How can I learn the fundamentals of electrical circuits and apply them to create a circuit solver website?
01

Learn

Develop an understanding of electrical circuits, voltage, current and resistance.

02

Develop

Apply programming skills to create an interactive and accessible website.

03

Share

Provide students with an educational tool that helps them solve electrical problems.

About CircuitLab

Connecting electrical engineering, programming and education.

About the Project

CircuitLab is an educational website designed to help students understand the fundamentals of electrical circuits and solve basic electrical equations.

This website was developed as part of my Capstone project to explore my interest in electrical engineering while improving my programming, mathematical and problem-solving skills.

Guiding Question

How can I learn the fundamentals of electrical circuits and apply them to create a circuit solver website?

My Motivation

I have been interested in mathematics, programming and electrical circuits. Through this project, I wanted to combine these interests and develop a practical educational tool.

This project also allows me to explore electrical engineering as a potential future career.
